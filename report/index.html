03THE NEXT CHECK

Review São Paulo's late orders in July 2018.

That month SP had 100 late deliveries among 2,712 eligible orders. Alagoas had 4 among 22, a higher rate on a far smaller base. Start with the SP orders and request carrier, route and service-promise data before naming a cause.
